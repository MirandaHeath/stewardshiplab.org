[NSOC](https://nsoc.engineering.nyu.edu/) is a new software supply chain security research lab embedded within NYU. Our
supervisors mentor a cohort of postgraduate students and guide them towards making impactful and respectful
contributions towards the security of specific Open Source ecosystems.

<h3>NSOC's pillars</h3>
<div class="program__features">
  <div class="program__feature">
    <h4>Connect</h4>
    <div>
      Students serve as dedicated liaisons to specific Open Source ecosystems. Before engaging as security
      researchers, each student undergoes structured onboarding in the norms and culture of their ecosystem.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="program__feature">
    <h4>Inspect</h4>
    <div>
      Students develop and extend fuzzing frameworks, LLM-assisted code review pipelines, and automated patch
      generation agents.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="program__feature">
    <h4>Detect</h4>
    <div>
      Continuous monitoring powered by
      <a href="https://ecosyste.ms" target="_blank">ecosyste.ms</a>,
      covering dependency changes, anomalous package behavior, suspicious injections, and unmaintained critical
      components. NSOC coordinates responsible disclosure through ecosystem liaisons.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="program__feature">
    <h4>Direct</h4>
    <div>
      Annual ecosystem report cards assess security posture across trusted publishing adoption, dependency
      hygiene, and vulnerability responsiveness. NSOC delivers direct code integrations and coordinates with
      OpenSSF, NIST, and ecosystem governance bodies.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
