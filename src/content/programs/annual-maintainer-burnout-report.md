Miranda Heath's Nov 2025
[report](/reports/burnout-in-open-source-a-structural-problem-we-can-fix-together/)
on burnout in Open Source amplified maintainers' stories about their struggles with burnout. She identified
causes of burnout including maintainers struggling to make a living and feeling pressured to work a stressful
“second shift” after their day job; and being worn down by entitled behaviour from community members.

Miranda is now working on an annual survey of Open Source maintainers, to understand the ongoing factors
affecting their well-being. This work is important and timely. Since the last Tidelift State of the Open
Source Maintainer survey in 2024, it appears nobody has been regularly collecting data on working conditions
in Open Source. This data is needed now more than ever, as the rise of AI coding is creating new pressures on
Open Source maintainers in urgent need of effective and data-driven solutions.

By contributing to this fund, you will help ensure that it is always possible to know how the Open Source
maintainers we rely on are doing, how this is evolving over time, and where they could most use our support.
