This page contains:

* Our **Website Privacy Policy**, which applies to visitors of our website.
* Our **Maintainer Council Privacy Policy**, which applies to those that sign up for the Maintainer Council.

These privacy policies explain how we collect, use and store your personal information. Personal information means any
information that could identify you.

If you need to contact us about your data, reach out to our Data Protection Officer. Our Data Protection Officer is Vlad
Miricioiu who can be contacted at [vlad@vlad.website](mailto:vlad@vlad.website).

If you have a complaint about our handling of your data, you can [make a complaint][complaint] with the Information
Commissioner’s Office.

## Website Privacy Policy

This section applies to visitors of our website.

**We do not collect any personal information.**

## Maintainer Council Privacy Policy

This section applies to people that sign up for the Maintainer Council.

**We collect your email address.**

This information is never displayed publicly. Your email address is used to get in touch with you about updates
regarding the Maintainer Council. Your information is not used for any other purposes and not shared with anyone else.

We keep the aforementioned information for the duration of your Maintainer Council membership. We delete your
information if you request to be removed as a member.

Our [lawful basis][lawful-basis] for collecting your information is _consent_. We have permission from you to collect
this data after we have given you all the relevant information. All of your [data protection rights][individual-rights]
may apply, except the right to object. To be clear, you do have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
