This page contains details about membership to the Software Stewardship Lab.

## Directors

Our board of directors is the governing body of the Lab. Our directors make decisions about how to run the Lab, and also
hold ultimate legal responsibility for the management of the non-profit as its
[officers](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/SC893294/officers).

We are not actively recruiting directors at this time.

## Advisers

Our advisers are trusted colleagues who regularly meet with us to give us their input on the Lab's activities. However,
they hold no formal governance or legal responsibility.

We are not actively recruiting advisers at this time.

## Researchers

Our researchers are regular collaborators who do work in affiliation with the Lab.

We are open to working with new researchers. For a person to become a researcher, there are two prerequisites:

- we must have collaborated with that person on a Lab-affiliated funded project; and
- that person must express interest in collaborating on future projects, and must be willing to develop funding
applications together with us.
