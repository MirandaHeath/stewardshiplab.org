We are excited to announce the Call for Participation for the Package Management devroom at FOSDEM 2027, taking place on
31 Jan 2027, from 13:15 to 17:00, at the Université libre de Bruxelles, Belgium.

## About the Devroom

Package managers are critical infrastructure for developers and end users alike, handling dependency resolution,
software supply chain security, reproducibility, provenance, and long-term maintainability.

The Free and Open Source community has built dozens of package managers across operating systems (_apt, pacman, Nix_),
programming language ecosystems (_PyPI, pip, uv, RubyGems, Conda, Pixi_), domain-specific tools (_Spack, EasyBuild for
HPC_), and universal formats (_Flatpak, Snap, AppImage_). Each has its unique trade-offs and innovations.

But as ecosystems proliferate, package managers face recurring challenges: dependency solving, namespace collisions,
supply chain security, phantom dependencies, reproducibility, metadata standards, cross-ecosystem dependencies, and
cryptographic signing of artifacts.

In this past year, new challenges have appeared: an unprecedented rise in package submissions due to LLM usage, and
novel supply chain attacks, amid systemic underfunding and shifting governance.

This devroom provides a gathering place for maintainers, researchers, and users of package managers to make more
progress together than they could have individually; a place to foster collaboration and discuss lessons learned, new
developments, and shared challenges.

## Submission Guidelines

We can't wait to see your submissions, at a time when package management and supply chain security are more important
than ever. We welcome talks on any topic that contains a substantial technical focus on package management and software
infrastructure.

We especially encourage submissions on under-discussed topics, and submissions from individuals and communities
underrepresented in Free and Open Source software. Topics that are frequently discussed elsewhere, such as augmenting
existing security approaches using AI, will be deprioritised. We also do not accept talks that primarily promote
products.
